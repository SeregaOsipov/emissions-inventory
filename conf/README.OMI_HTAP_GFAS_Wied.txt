This setup combines OMI-HTAP and HTAP.

OMI-HTAP updated the SO2 emissions.
Thus, I'm still using original HTAP for all species except SO2 and take SO2 from OMI-HTAP for 4 sectors: energy, industry, residential, transport.
Ship and Air sectors of HTAP are used as is.

Speciation E002-E004 do not have SO2
